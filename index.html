Explore. Question. Grow.

Where kids learn to ask the big questions.

Camp Quest UK is a summer camp for children and young people. It's somewhere to belong, ask why, and try things you didn't think you could. Big questions get taken seriously, and everyone gets listened to.

What we do

Come for the big questions. Stay for your people.

Camp Quest is a place to belong, get curious and try new things. Children talk through difficult topics, learn to disagree kindly, and find out why.

Science

We learn with evidence and the scientific method. “It just is” isn't an answer.

Philosophy

Philosophy for children. We talk questions through together instead of handing out answers.

Outdoors

Physical activities and group challenges. There's always support if it feels scary.

Conversation

Listening, taking turns, changing your mind. You're taken seriously, whatever your age.

Big questions

What we hope campers say afterwards.

I found my people. I felt included as I am.

I learnt something I never would have at school. People took my opinions seriously.

I did something scary, and it was so cool. I felt brave.

I could share my thoughts and no one laughed.

At camp

Something for everyone.

Every camp has a theme, and a mix of thinking, making and moving about.

  • Philosophy for children and safe spaces to discuss
  • Physical activities and group bonding
  • Drama games, with permission to be silly
  • Creative time
  • Group challenges
  • Learning new things around a theme
